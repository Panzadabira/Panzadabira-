# 👋 Hello, I'm Jonathan Barenco (Panzadabira)

Indie game developer, sports instructor, and founder of **[Panza Labs](https://panzadabira.itch.io/)**. 
I build tools that remove the friction from game development, specializing in **LLM-driven architectural analysis** and **automated CI/CD workflows**.

---

### 🧬 The Panza Labs Philosophy
Panza Labs was born in August 2025 from a simple belief: **Game development should be about creativity, not battling broken tools.** I started as a player, fell in love with complex mechanics and tabletop gaming, and realized that the industry needed better plumbing. From rapid prototyping to complex multi-agent AI systems, I document my journey into building high-end DevTools that solve real-world architectural headaches. My tools are born from my own indie gamedev journey—tested in the trenches of projects like *Wildroots* and *SaniDex*.

---

### 🛠️ Flagship Product: Atlas Codex
**[Atlas Codex](https://github.com/Panzadabira/AtlasCodex)** is a universal polyglot static analysis engine designed to perform zero-latency architectural mapping of complex codebases.

* **[Atlas Codex (Engine)](https://github.com/Panzadabira/AtlasCodex):** Open-source static analysis engine for polyglot codebases (Python/Standalone).
* **[Atlas Codex (Unity Extension)](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/slug/380466)**: Native C#/UI Toolkit integration for professional Unity pipelines. Available for **42.00 CHF**.
